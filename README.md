# Hi there, I'm Reza 👋

**Senior Hardware Engineer | Hardware Systems Architect** | Power Electronics, Analog & PCB Design, Motor Drives, Embedded Systems & EMC.

7+ years in industry, taking electronics from the first schematic to mass production. Currently Hardware Systems Architect at ML!PA Consulting GmbH in Berlin.


---


### ⚡ Technical Expertise

* **Power Electronics & Topologies:** SMPS (Buck, Boost, SEPIC, Flyback, Push-Pull, PFC), Half- and Full-Bridge Three-Phase Drivers, Motor Drives (PMSM, BLDC, FOC), Gate Drives (SiC / GaN / MOSFET), Magnetics Design, from 1.8 V rails up to 6 kV
* **Analog & Measurement:** Precision and Low-Leakage Front Ends down to the picoampere range, Mixed-Signal Design, Sensor Interfacing, Battery Management
* **Circuit Design & Simulation:** LTspice, PSpice, PLECS, SIMetrix, MATLAB/Simulink
* **PCB Design & Layout:** Altium Designer, KiCad (Multilayer, High-Speed, RF, High-Current / High-Voltage, EMC-conscious layout, DFM)
* **Embedded Systems:** C / C++ on Infineon AURIX and STM32, FreeRTOS and Bare Metal, Low-Level Drivers, Bootloaders, OTA Updates
* **Validation & Testing:** EMC/EMI Pre-compliance and Accredited-Lab Compliance Testing, Spectrum and Network Analysis, High-Power Bench Bring-up & Debugging
